# Marketing content workflow

Use this when the owner wants research, a draft, a platform-specific version, a review packet, a publish or schedule step they have already approved, or a performance summary.

## Inputs

Confirm the campaign goal, audience, destination account, deliverable, and which assets are approved for this item. If one of those is missing, ask one question and wait. A content calendar entry, a saved preference, or permission to install a skill is not permission to publish.

## Research

Check factual claims against primary sources. Keep the source link with each claim. Label a statement as verified, vendor-claimed, or uncertain. Do not invent endorsements, affiliations, results, audience numbers, or rights to media.

## Draft and adapt

Draft in the voice the owner set. Adapt format, length, caption, title, accessibility text, and links to the destination's current limits. Preserve the meaning of the approved copy. Create a visual only when the owner asks for one, and confirm asset rights and any AI disclosure that applies.

Leave supplied media intact. Do not crop, shorten, reframe, or re-encode it unless the owner asks for that specific change.

## Review

Show a review packet before any send or schedule. For each item include the destination account, exact copy, media, disclosures, cross-sharing state, and proposed time with timezone. A batch approval counts only when every included item and destination is listed. Use the product's draft and review mechanism when it is available. Publish or schedule only the exact action the owner then approves.

## Publish

Discover the tools that are actually available, then use the channel setup and publishing skills that match the destination. A missing connector upload does not prove that a permitted native upload is impossible, and it does not permit a browser sign-in the current rules forbid. Test only steps the owner has authorized. Leave safety blocks in place. Before retrying a send whose result is unclear, check the destination for a duplicate.

## Verify

Separate these states in the report: drafted, uploaded or private, scheduled, published, blocked, and cross-post not yet verified. Return a live link only after you have opened it and checked the destination identity and the content. For Facebook, a sharing toggle shows only that sharing was requested. Verify the separate Facebook copy before you say it published.

## Measure

When the owner asks for analytics, name the source, the date range, and the timezone. Separate measured results from hypotheses. Do not promise growth, reach, or outcomes.

## Boundaries

Do not send unsolicited replies, direct messages, or engagement. Do not spend money or change account settings. Keep campaign details and credentials out of shared skills and out of the public repository. Report the error you observed and the check you ran.
