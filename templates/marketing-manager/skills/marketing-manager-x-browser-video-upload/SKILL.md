# X video upload from an already-signed-in composer

Load the current desktop-browser guidance and the current x-api-mcp-guide before you start. Those current rules win over this file.

Use this only for an X session that is already signed in and currently permitted. Do not sign into x.com or console.x.com. Do not ask for credentials. Do not copy or create a session. Do not treat a connector error, a missing upload tool, or a refused scope as permission to switch to the browser.

## Confirm the assignment

Confirm the exact video the owner authorized, the intended account, the exact caption if there is one, and whether to stop after the video is attached. Attaching a video is not publication. This file does not grant sign-in permission or publishing permission.

## Open the composer

Use the supported desktop interaction. Open the X composer, read the handle on screen, and confirm it is the approved account. Leave unrelated drafts untouched. If a post might already have been sent, check the profile for a duplicate before trying again.

## Choose the video

Use the visible control labeled "Add photos or video". A hidden file input that fails does not mean the visible control fails. In the native picker, select only the authorized video. For a picker-only check, cancel without selecting a file.

## Wait and read the preview

Wait for upload and processing. Read the preview, duration, filename, readiness, and the exact error text if one appears. Do not say the video is ready until the interface says it is ready. Account and media limits can differ. Keep any evidence free of secrets and free of unrelated account data.

## Stop or publish

For attachment-only work, stop and leave the composer intact.

For publication, ask for approval of that exact account, video, and caption, then post once. If the outcome is unclear, inspect the profile before any retry. Open the live permalink and verify the account, the caption, and the video.

## Report

Report one observed result: the picker opened, the video is ready but unpublished, the post is published and the live permalink was verified, or the exact blocker on screen. A missing connector upload, by itself, is not a browser limitation.
