# Full-length video on YouTube or Instagram

Confirm the original media, each intended account, the approved title or caption, and the authorization for each destination. This file does not promise that every length, account, or platform will accept the upload.

Discover the live connector support first. A route that only accepts Shorts is not proof that the platform rejects a full-length video, and it is not proof that a full-length upload is available. Where a native upload is permitted, load the current desktop-browser guidance, the current sign-in guidance, and the guidance for that service, then use the supported browser interaction. Leave unrelated drafts untouched, check for a duplicate before a retry, and verify each account identity.

## Preserve the source

Read the source metadata. Keep the duration, aspect ratio, and framing. Do not trim, crop, or re-encode the file unless the owner asks for that specific change. Check the current native acceptance you can see. Do not promise that an arbitrary length works on every account.

## YouTube

Verify the intended Studio channel. When a standard video upload is supported, use that upload rather than a Short. Use the approved title and description inside the current limits. Set the audience from the actual content. Turn on the altered or synthetic disclosure that applies. Do not invent a paid-promotion status.

Check the full duration, processing, checks, and settings. Leave the video Private until the owner gives the required publication approval. When they approve publication, publish that existing upload instead of uploading the file again. Open the public watch page and confirm the channel and the video. A one-second difference in how the page rounds duration is not, by itself, proof the file was trimmed.

## Instagram

Verify the intended handle. Select the original media. Choose Original framing when it is offered, and keep the full trim range. Use the approved caption and the AI label that applies.

Leave Facebook sharing off unless the owner has opted in for the intended Facebook destination. Sharing to the Instagram feed is not Facebook cross-sharing. Stop before Share until the owner approves that publication. Share once. If the dialog stalls, inspect the profile before you try again. Open the live permalink and verify the caption, the label, and the video.

## Review and report

Show the exact destinations, title or caption, media, disclosure, and sharing settings under the product's current outgoing-message rules. Permission to upload is not permission to publish.

Return verified live URLs. Keep private, prepared, and public states distinct. A Facebook sharing control shows only that it was enabled. Verify the separate Facebook copy before you say that copy was published.

If the upload is unsupported or blocked, report the facts you observed and the supported next step. Leave safeguards in place.
